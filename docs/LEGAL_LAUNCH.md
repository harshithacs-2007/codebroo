# CodeBroo Legal & IP Launch Checklist

This is an engineering/product checklist, not legal advice.

## Product and IP

- Keep CodeBroo's UI, illustrations, pet designs, copy, lesson content and code original or properly licensed.
- Record the license and attribution for every third-party package, font, icon, image, dataset and model.
- Keep a software bill of materials or dependency inventory.
- Do not copy Duolingo, LeetCode, Wayground or another product's distinctive artwork, copy, layouts, mascots or branding. Use them only as product inspiration.
- Before commercial launch, perform a formal trademark clearance for “CodeBroo”, the logo, pet names and any future product names in the jurisdictions where the company will operate. A general web search is not trademark clearance.

## Privacy and data minimisation

CodeBroo should collect the minimum learner data required to provide the service.

Avoid collecting:
- unnecessary real names;
- precise location;
- contacts;
- microphone/camera data unless a feature explicitly needs them;
- raw code history forever;
- unnecessary behavioral telemetry.

Define retention periods for attempts, logs, AI conversations and account data. Provide account/data deletion mechanisms appropriate to the markets served.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 were notified on 14 November 2025, with staged commencement provisions, so the launch checklist should be mapped to the applicable implementation dates and your actual processing activities.

## Children

Do not market CodeBroo as an unrestricted children's service until the child-privacy flow is legally reviewed.

If the service is directed to children or you have actual knowledge that you are collecting covered children's personal information, child-privacy requirements can apply. COPPA is relevant to covered U.S. services involving children under 13. In the EU, the GDPR has additional rules for children's consent in information-society services, with the relevant threshold varying by Member State.

Build age-appropriate privacy notices, parental-consent/age-assurance flows where legally required, retention controls and deletion/access mechanisms before deliberately targeting minors.

## AI pet

The AI buddy should clearly be presented as an AI system, not a human tutor.

Before launch, document:
- what learner data the AI sees;
- what is stored;
- whether prompts/outputs are used for model training;
- which external AI providers receive data;
- how users can delete stored AI conversations;
- safety rules and escalation behavior;
- limits on academic/legal/medical/other high-stakes advice.

Do not send secrets, session tokens, database records or unrelated learner data into model prompts.

## Commercial readiness

Before taking payment or signing institutional contracts:
- form the business entity with professional advice;
- review founder/IP ownership and contributor agreements;
- publish Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Acceptable Use Policy;
- document refunds/cancellations if paid;
- arrange a security/contact channel;
- consider appropriate cyber and general liability insurance;
- have counsel review the final contracts and policies.

## Evidence trail

Keep dated records of:
- dependency licenses;
- original design/source ownership;
- trademark searches;
- security test results;
- penetration-test findings and remediation;
- privacy/data-flow diagrams;
- AI provider agreements;
- policy versions and approval dates.
